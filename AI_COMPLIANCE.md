# AI Compliance Statement
v0.2.0

## Project Name
fishFingers - BCF Prediction Model

## Overview
This repository contains a machine learning model developed to predict the bioaccumulation potential of chemical substances (e.g., Bioconcentration Factor, BCF) using curated experimental data and chemical structural descriptors/fingerprints. The project has been created for **research, development, educational, and training purposes**.

## Intended Use
The model is intended to support:

- Scientific research in environmental chemistry, toxicology, and cheminformatics  
- Screening and prioritisation of chemicals for further investigation  
- Method development in predictive modelling  
- Educational demonstration of machine learning workflows  
- Training in data science applications for chemical datasets  

## Prohibited Uses
This system is **not intended** for use as the sole basis for:

- Regulatory enforcement decisions  
- Legal determinations  
- Human health diagnosis or treatment  
- Employment, credit, insurance, or social scoring decisions  
- Safety-critical automated decision-making  
- Any use affecting the rights or freedoms of natural persons  

## Risk Classification
Based on the current intended use and available understanding of the **EU AI Act** and similar emerging regulatory frameworks, this project is considered a **minimal-risk / low-risk AI system**, as it:

- Does not process personal data  
- Does not profile individuals  
- Does not perform biometric identification  
- Does not make decisions affecting natural persons  
- Is limited to scientific prediction of chemical properties  

If deployed in another context, risk classification may change and should be reassessed by the deploying organisation.

## Human Oversight
Outputs from this model are predictive estimates only and require interpretation by qualified users. Human review is necessary before using results in any scientific, commercial, or regulatory context.

## Data Governance
This project is developed using chemical and scientific datasets only. No personal data is intentionally collected, processed, or inferred.

Reasonable efforts should be made to ensure:

- Lawful use of source datasets  
- Appropriate licensing compliance  
- Data quality review  
- Transparent preprocessing workflows  
- Version control of datasets and models  

## Technical Limitations
Model predictions may be less reliable when:

- Chemicals fall outside the training domain  
- Novel chemistry is encountered  
- Input descriptors are incomplete or incorrect  
- Historical training data contains variability or uncertainty  

Users should consider applicability domain assessment and independent validation where appropriate.
